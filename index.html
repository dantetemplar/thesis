Optimization

Problem Formulation (CB-CTT)

  • Input: CB-CTT model (courses, groups, instructors, rooms, grid)
  • Decision variables: day, slot, instructor, room per meeting
  • Hard: no overlaps, capacity, banned slots
  • Soft: 12 normalized penalty terms → next slides

Optimization

Soft Objective Function

\[ \min \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left\lfloor \frac{w_i \cdot K}{D_i} \right\rceil p_i \]
\(n\)
soft penalty terms (\(n = 12\), phase 1)
\(p_i\)
penalty count; \(0 \le p_i \le D_i\)
\(D_i\)
worst-case bound for \(p_i\)
\(w_i\)
term weight (\(w_i = 1\))
\(K\)
scale constant (\(K = 10^{4}\))
\(\lfloor \cdot \rceil\)
nearest-integer rounding

Optimization — Objective

Pedagogical coherence

1

Same-day lec + tut + lab

Lecture, tutorial & lab on one day per (course, group)

p = T − # satisfied triples

2

Adjacent lec → tut

Tutorial immediately after lecture, same day

p = P − # satisfied pairs

3

Adjacent lec → tut, same room

Back-to-back lec & tut without a room change

p = P − # same-room pairs

4

Room utilization

Prefer smallest suitable room — avoid >30% oversize when a smaller one fits

p = # oversize picks

5

Wrong component order

Avoid lab before lecture or tutorial; avoid tutorial before lecture

p = # reversed meeting pairs

Optimization — Objective

Student experience

6

Meetings overload

At most 5 meetings per group per day

p = # overloaded (group, day) flags

7

Distinct subjects overload

At most 3 distinct subjects per group per day

p = # days with ≥4 subjects

8

Weekend (Sat / Sun)

No classes on weekend days

p = # weekend meetings

9

After 18:00

No meetings in slots ending after 18:00

p = # late-slot meetings

10

Group spread

Compact week — active days ≤ ⌈weekly / 5⌉ per group

p = Σ excessg

Instructor experience

11

Instructor spread

Compact teaching week per instructor

p = Σ excessi (assignment-aware)

12

Instructor preferences

Penalize discouraged weekday+slot placements; banned cells are hard-forbidden

p = Σ weighted discouraged violations (× role multiplier)

Optimization — Phase 2

Room Choice Refinement

Separate CP-SAT solver run after phase 1 — day, slot, and instructor fixed; only rooms are reassigned.

1

Back-to-back lec → tut room mismatch

Adjacent lecture & tutorial on the same day — unify rooms when phase 1 placed them apart

p = # diff-room pairs (same-room pairs hard-fixed)

2

Room utilization

Prefer smallest suitable room — no regression from a non-oversize phase 1 pick

p = # oversize picks

3

Consecutive class / lab room swap

Same instructor, same day, back-to-back class or lab — avoid changing rooms

p = # consecutive room mismatches

Unsafe in phase 1 — the solver can avoid these penalties by spreading meetings to different days, abusing the objective.
